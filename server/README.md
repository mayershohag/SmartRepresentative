# Smart Representative

Smart Representative is a multi-distributor B2B commerce and distribution management platform designed to modernize the traditional sales representative-based ordering system.

The platform allows distributors to manage companies, products, inventory, sales representatives, delivery personnel, and shopkeepers from a centralized system.

Instead of relying entirely on traditional sales representatives to visit shops and collect orders manually, Smart Representative aims to provide a digital ordering and distribution workflow where shopkeepers can easily find products, place orders, and receive their purchases through the distributor's delivery network.

## Core Concept

Company → Products → Distributor → Sales Representative → Shopkeeper → Order → Delivery

## Main Features

- Multi-distributor architecture
- Company and product management
- Product categories
- Distributor-specific product pricing and inventory
- Sales Representative management
- Delivery Man management
- Shopkeeper management
- Product ordering system
- Order management and tracking
- Inventory management
- Role-based authentication and authorization
- Distributor-specific product availability
- Future support for offers, discounts, credit, payments, and analytics

## User Roles

- Distributor
- Sales Representative (SR)
- Delivery Man
- Shopkeeper
- Admin (planned)

## Project Vision

The long-term goal of Smart Representative is to create a scalable B2B distribution ecosystem where multiple distributors and companies can manage their products and supply chains digitally while allowing shopkeepers to order products quickly and efficiently.

This project is currently under active development.
